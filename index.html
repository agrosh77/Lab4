Coffee Production in Ecuador, 2025

About this map

This interactive choropleth map shows reported coffee production by province in Ecuador in 2025. Provinces are shaded from pale yellow to dark blue, with darker shades representing greater coffee production. Gray indicates that no coffee production data were reported for that province. Users can select individual provinces to view production totals, switch between topographic and satellite basemaps, and display a NASA elevation layer for additional geographic context.

Data source: Coffee production data from Ecuador's Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock, based on INEC ESPAC agricultural statistics. View source data .
